The legend

The wheel has turned again

Sailors say the Kaalchakra was carved from a single banyan and bound in brass. Turn it and your ship doesn't change course. It changes hour.

Last season 186 crews went after it. 24 reached Goa. One crew, root@void, brought it home. Now the wheel has spun once more and scattered its flags across eight seas.

Captain Kaal is recruiting, and he isn't the only pirate after those flags. Click him if you have questions, or open the doubloon counter in the bottom-left corner and press Talk to the captain.

Read before you sail

The Pirate Code

  1. Open to UG and PG students enrolled at recognised Indian institutions. Every member needs a valid college ID.
  2. Crews of one to three. Each person sails with one crew only, and members can't be swapped after registration.
  3. No sharing of flags or solutions between crews. Everyone involved walks the plank (gets disqualified).
  4. Attack the challenges, never the infrastructure: no DoS, no attacks on the scoreboard, and no brute-forcing or scanners unless a challenge asks for it.
  5. Flags look like Kaal{s0m3_t3xt_h3r3} unless a challenge says otherwise.
  6. Finalists arrange their own travel and stay. Cash prizes are subject to TDS. The organisers' decision on disputes is final.

These are the 2026 rules carried forward. The final 2.0 rules will be published when registration opens.
